Privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Pocket Stockroom ("we") is an inventory app for small businesses, operated from Montréal, Québec. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. It is written to meet Québec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25) and Canada's PIPEDA.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address and a password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Business data you enter: products, stock counts, lots, suppliers, purchase orders, invoices and sales figures. This is your business's information; we hold it for you.
  • Team membership: which accounts belong to which business, and their role (owner or staff).
  • Technical data: basic server logs (time, IP address, type of request) kept for security and troubleshooting.

Photos you take of invoices, sales reports and products are read on your phone. Product photos stay on your device and are not uploaded. If you choose to add your own AI provider key for invoice reading, invoice images are sent to that provider under your account with them.

Why we use it

  • To let you sign in and keep your stock in sync across your team's phones.
  • To send account emails (confirmation, password reset, and notice of changes to plans or this policy).
  • To keep the service secure and working.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or look at your business data except when you ask us for help or the law requires it.

Where it is stored

Account and business data is stored with our hosting provider Supabase in its Canada (Central) region, in Montréal. The website itself is served through a global content network. Some service providers may process limited technical data outside Québec; we only use providers that protect it to an equivalent standard.

Who can see it

Only members of your business, and only after the business owner invites them. Each business's data is separated at the database level so other customers cannot read it.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account is open. When you delete your account in the app (More → Account → Delete my account), your login is deleted immediately; if you are the owner, the business and all its stock data are deleted too. Backups held by our provider expire within 30 days.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export (More → Export to Excel or Back up) and delete your information at any time, withdraw consent, and file a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.

Person in charge of personal information

Changes

If we make a meaningful change we will email account owners and update the date above.

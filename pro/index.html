One day in the stockroom.

Built by someone who runs a receiving dock in Montréal, around the four jobs that eat a small team's day.

  1. 7 am · delivery

    Receive

    Photograph the invoice. Lines, quantities and lot numbers are read for you and matched to your products.

  2. 8:30 am · shelf

    Stock the counter

    Move cases from storage to the counter with a scan. Storage, counter and the overall total stay separate.

  3. 6 pm · close

    Deduct sales

    Import the POS sales report as a PDF or photo. Packs come off by the pack, deli by the kilo.

  4. Weekly

    Reorder

    Low and expiring items rise to the top. Send a purchase order to the supplier by email in two taps.

Questions shop owners ask.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Open the app link in Safari or Chrome, then use Share → Add to Home Screen. It opens full-screen like any other app and works offline.

Which POS systems work with the sales import?

Any POS that can export a sales report by product as a PDF. The first time, you link each report line to a product; after that the app remembers. Photos of printed reports work too, but a PDF is exact.

Can I bring my existing product list?

Yes — scan products as you go, add them straight from an invoice or sales report, or restore from a Pocket Stockroom backup file.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

You keep read access and can export everything to Excel or a backup file. We don't delete a paid account's data without warning you first.

Who can see my stock?

Only the people you invite. Each business's data is locked to its own members, stored in Canada, and never sold or shared. Read the privacy policy.
